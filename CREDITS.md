## Original content

- **2D art** (unit and hero sprites, portraits, UI, icons, items, campaign illustrations, cursors): made for
  this project with AI image generation (OpenAI) and the project's own palette, outline and pixel pipeline.
- **3D models** (units, heroes, workers, buildings, settlements, walls, towers, landmarks `assets/props3d/lm_*`):
  generated with Tripo (tripo3d.ai) from the project's own reference art, then converted and recoloured.
- **Animation**: character animations retargeted from Adobe Mixamo (mixamo.com) clips. They are used
  under Adobe's Mixamo terms, embedded in the game's models; they are not offered as standalone files.
- **Music and sounds**: synthesized from code (the main game's `tools/audio/synth.py`); no samples or
  third-party recordings.
- **Code, rules, story and data**: original.
