# Generation notes

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool from the supplied prompts in prompts.json.
Existing portraits and unit sprites were supplied as identity references. New classes used their written designs.

After the first Knight draft, the arm-spacing instruction was strengthened to request arms angled 35 degrees from vertical, with fists clear of hips and continuous background gaps.

User feedback: make future characters more unique. Subsequent hero prompts emphasized differences in physique, garment construction, head shape, signature features and material palettes, while retaining each class portrait identity and faction colours. Rigging constraints remained: empty hands, separated limbs, short hems, no mounts and no free particles.

Dreadmarshal's loose hair was gathered into a compact knot and Broodbinder's ponytail was shortened above the shoulders after review. The exact correction prompts are recorded in generation-notes.json.

No models or rigs were generated in this batch.
